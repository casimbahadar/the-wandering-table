- 2026-07-29 — Leveling model CONFIRMED SRD-native (Claude Code verified against the PDF; design call by Casim + chat): the milestone-as-XP-grant unification is not a workaround — SRD *Level Advancement* (p.23) is the only advancement system, XP awards are explicitly "awarded by the Game Master" and not restricted to combat kills, so a fixed XP lump at an act break IS the SRD's own system. Milestone advancement itself is NOT in SRD 5.2 (DMG material) — the app must never cite it as SRD-derived, and player-facing text says XP, never "milestone."
- 2026-07-29 — Recruitment XP is license-clean (Claude, inferred from verified SRD text): "XP is awarded for defeating the monster in combat or otherwise neutralizing it" covers sparing/recruiting and peaceful resolution. - 2026-07-29 — Campaign shape (Casim): hub-and-spoke is the structure — a home base with regions/quests available in varying order, growing out of camp (already the rest/level-up/save beat) — with open-world elements folded in opportunistically wherever they fit.
- 2026-07-29 — Admissibility rule for open-world elements (Claude PROPOSED, so future sessions can decide without asking): ADMIT free movement within a region, optional off-path locations, multiple valid orders within an act, and persistent minor world flags — anything that still resolves into budget-verified encounters and a walkable scene graph. - 2026-07-29 — Tutorial continuity (Casim): the tutorial is the campaign character's FIRST JOB, not a throwaway pregen — creation happens before it, and its outcomes (companion recruited or not, injuries, potion, name) persist into Act 1. - 2026-07-29 — Class roster ambition (Casim): all SRD 5.2 classes AND subclasses available from launch, for maximum replayability from day one — not phased in over time (supersedes the 2026-07-28 "over time" framing in direction, though sequencing remains the production plan's job).

Recommendation on the table: bound Campaign 1's level cap (rec: 5), ship classes in waves with a quality gate (brain + sims + playtest), and verify against the PDF how many subclasses SRD 5.2 actually contains before promising "all subclasses."
- 2026-07-29 — CORRECTION to Claude's earlier scoping line (superseding, per append-only discipline): "levels 1–5 = core kit plus a couple of features" was WRONG. The comparative claim survives (1–5 is a small fraction of 1–20); the absolute per-class cost does not. Unit of work is 24 kits (12 classes + 12 subclasses), not 12.
- 2026-07-29 — Level-5 cap recommendation STANDS and is strengthened (Claude): subclass is gained at level 3 in all 12 classes uniformly, so a level-5 cap includes the subclass choice — the "all classes and subclasses" promise is not hollow at that cap. Awaiting Casim's confirm.
- 2026-07-29 — Guided-brain architecture (Claude PROPOSED, the lever that makes the full roster affordable): build ~5 ARCHETYPE brains (melee striker, ranged striker, healer/support, controller, skirmisher) driven by per-class ABILITY DATA (damage / heal / control / buff, cost, uses, range, conditions) instead of 24 bespoke brains. Converts the roster's real multiplier from 24 AI implementations into 5 brains + 24 data files, consistent with the data-driven direction of recruitment and campaigns. Ship = test still holds: the harness sims each class through its archetype brain. Specialize with a per-class override ONLY where sims or playtest show the generic brain playing that class badly.
